Who we are

Small crew. Real ops. No agency bloat.

We started as four freelancers splitting client work over group chats. Eighteen months later we're seven people with fixed roles — a lead, two developers, two people who own client relationships and HR, one who runs marketing, and one who handles general support so nothing falls through the cracks between desks.

No office, no fixed hours, no ping-pong table. Just a roster, a shared calendar, and a way of working that scales past "just a few of us doing favors for each other."

How we work

From first call to handover

The same four steps for every client, so nothing depends on catching the right person at the right time.

01
Discovery call
You talk to OPS first — they scope what you actually need before anyone quotes a price.
02
Scope & quote
A fixed quote and timeline, agreed before DEV or MKT touch a single deliverable.
03
Build with check-ins
Regular updates from whoever's building — no disappearing for three weeks and resurfacing with a surprise.
04
Handover & support
You get the finished work plus a support window, handled by your OPS contact — not a ticket queue.
