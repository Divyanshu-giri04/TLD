Who We Are

Small crew. Real ops. No agency bloat.

We started as four freelancers splitting client work over group chats. Eighteen months later we're seven people with fixed roles — a lead, two developers, two people who own client relationships and HR, one who runs marketing, and one who handles general support so nothing falls through the cracks between desks.

No office, no fixed hours, no ping-pong table. Just a roster, a shared calendar, and a way of working that scales past "just a few of us doing favors for each other."
